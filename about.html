About Us

Professional electrical solutions built on quality, honesty and reliability.

Mkhonkosi Electrical Services (Pty) Ltd

Based in Ermelo, Mpumalanga, Mkhonkosi Electrical Services (Pty) Ltd provides dependable electrical services for residential, commercial and industrial clients throughout the province.

We believe in quality workmanship, honest communication, safe working practices and customer satisfaction. Every project is completed with professionalism and attention to detail, no matter the size.

From new electrical installations and maintenance to solar solutions and fault finding, our experienced team is committed to delivering reliable services that meet the highest standards.
